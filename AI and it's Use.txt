Ai and its use

For Study Purpose:
1.Quizlet AI:

Best For: Exam Prep, Memorisation & Flashcards. The classic flashcard platform completely overhauled with modern generative AI features ("Q-Chat").

2. NotebookLM (notebooklm.google.com):


 The Key Feature: It generates customized mock exams and study guides grounded only in your uploaded material. It also generates a downloadable "Audio Overview" (a 2-person podcast) discussing your syllabus, allowing you to listen and revise while commuting to the exam hall

3.Astra AI (astra-ai.co):

 Astra measures your understanding continuously by quizzing you. If it notices you are consistently failing questions about a specific formula or historic date, it dynamically adjusts its difficulty, flags that topic, and forces you to repeat it until you master it before the actual exam date.

4.Quizgecko (quizgecko.com):


 Paste your massive revision text blocks or upload a PDF. The AI isolates every testable fact and builds an interactive exam interface complete with detailed explanations for why an answer was right or wrong.


5.Mindgrasp (mindgrasp.ai):

 It processes the massive files and outputs an Exam Review Sheet, breaking down only the highly testable definitions, core formulas, and crucial arguments so you don't waste time on filler content.

6.ChatGPT (://openai.com):

It has the most adaptable conversational memory and access to advanced "thinking models" (o1/o3) for tough subjects.

7.Google Gemini (://google.com):

 It is connected directly to Google Search, meaning it can pull up real, historical past exam papers from the internet.

8.Anthropic Claude (claude.ai):

It is the absolute best at spotting logical flaws in your writing and breaking down complex text.

9.Microsoft Copilot (://microsoft.com):

It is built directly into Microsoft Edge and Windows, making it incredibly easy to access while reading PDFs.

10.Socratic by Google:

Take a picture of your physical textbook problem or handwritten worksheet using your phone camera.
The AI immediately scans the question and pulls up highly visual explanations, YouTube video breakdowns, and curated study guides to teach you the underlying concept.


For Research Purpose:

1.Semantic Scholar:

Search millions of academic research papers.
Get AI-generated paper summaries.
Explore related research and citations.
Understand what a paper is about before reading it.

2.Google Scholar: (IT IS NOT AI TOOL BUT ESSENTIAL FOR ACADEMIC RESEARCH)

Find journal articles, conference papers and theses.
Check how many times a paper has been cited.
Find related research and authors.
Discover research from universities worldwide.

3.Elicit:

Search relevant research papers using questions.
 Summarize multiple research papers.
 Extract important information into tables.
 Compare findings from different studies.
Very useful when preparing the literature review section of a research paper.

4. Consensus:

Ask questions and receive answers based on academic papers.
Find supporting research for your arguments.
Compare the findings of different studies.
Get citations to the underlying research.
Free plan includes unlimited paper searches, 10 Pro messages and 3 Deep Reviews per month.

5. ResearchRabbit:

Find papers related to your selected paper.
Explore connections between authors and studies.
Visualize citation networks.
Organize papers into collections.
Free tier supports up to 50 starting articles and unlimited searches.

6. SciSpace:

Upload a research paper in PDF format.
Ask questions about the paper.
Understand complicated equations and technical terms.
Summarize research methodology and results.
Particularly useful when reading complex AIML research papers.

7. NotebookLM(free plan with limits):

Upload multiple research papers.
Ask questions based on your uploaded sources.
Generate summaries and study notes.
Connect information from different documents.
Create audio overviews and other study materials.
The free allowance currently includes up to 50 sources per notebook.

8. ChatGPT(free plan with limits)

**DeepSearch**


Best for research assistance and paper structure
Brainstorm research topics.
Develop research questions and objectives.
Prepare a research-paper outline.
Explain research methodologies.
Analyze datasets and help interpret results.
Review the clarity and structure of your draft.
You can also upload PDFs and ask questions about them.

9.Paperpal:


Best for academic writing and grammar
Improve academic English.
Correct grammar and sentence structure.
Rewrite sentences in an academic tone.
Help prepare abstracts and paper outlines.
Provide writing and reference-checking assistance.
Free plan includes 200 language suggestions per month and up to 5 uses per day of writing features.

10.Zotero:

Best for citations and reference management
Save research papers and their details.
Organize references into folders.
Generate citations in APA, IEEE and other formats.
Automatically create bibliographies in Word and Google Docs.
Zotero is not an AI chatbot, but it is one of the most useful tools for producing a properly referenced research paper. It provides 300 MB of free cloud file storage.
